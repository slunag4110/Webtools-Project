OUR STORY

Our History

Founded by Rodolfo Española Jr. in 1997, Youth on the Rock (YTR) began with a vision inspired by a newspaper article highlighting the potential for change in the Philippines after 100 years. His love for the youth and his desire to improve the nation's future led him to establish an organization dedicated to making a difference. YTR started by mentoring college students from the Polytechnic University of the Philippines – Sta. Mesa and eventually expanded to other campuses through Change Movement activities.

Over the years, YTR's pioneering members have become catalysts for positive change in their workplaces and communities, exemplifying the organization's principles of purpose-driven living, personal growth, and leadership development. YTR believes in empowering young leaders to become agents of change, not only in the future but also in the present. Grounded in the belief that everyone has a purpose, the organization helps young people overcome struggles, reach their potential, and make meaningful contributions to their campuses, communities, and nation. It also emphasizes the importance of building healthy relationships with others and with God as essential to personal growth and fulfilling one's purpose.
